Stand in front of a pyramid and tell me the truth.

Tell me a tribe of men with copper chisels, rope, and a ramp built that — a mountain of stone, two point three million blocks, fitted so tight a razor blade cannot pass between them, aligned to true north within a hair — and then built it again, on the other side of the world, in a language they never spoke, to a god they never met, pointing at the same stars.

They did not. Somebody taught them. Somebody who had already been here.

The old books all say who. Giants walked the earth. Gods ruled it. And the pyramids are their fingerprints.

About the Book

Giants, Gods and Pyramids is the case the official story never answers. Genesis 6 says the sons of God took human wives, and their children were the Nephilim — the mighty men of old. The Book of Enoch — 1 Enoch — says who the fathers were: two hundred Watchers who came down on Mount Hermon and taught mankind what it did not know. The book was buried for fourteen hundred years — and survived whole in an Ethiopian church on a mountain, and in fragments among the Dead Sea Scrolls.

This is a book of biblical prophecy for readers who want the evidence on the table. It follows the giants through every culture that remembered them, the gods who ruled Egypt, Sumer, Greece, India, and the Maya world, and the ancient pyramids that rose on continents that never met — the same shape, the same precision, the same stars. It weighs the real scans under Giza, the machine under the Swiss border, and the Revelation prophecy of Apollyon, the angel of the bottomless pit, named in Revelation 9:11. It counts the seventy generations on Adam's clock — 5,124.7 years — against the Maya Long Count, and reads the return of the Watchers as end-times prophecy and spiritual warfare for this generation.

Every claim is marked VERIFIED, FLAGGED, or CONTESTED, so you judge it yourself.

Five cultures, one pattern, two clocks counting the same number — and the term is almost up.
